## Section 8: Three Testable Predictions
1. ALLOSTATIC: Combined stressor noise tolerance satisfies
   tau_12 <= min(tau_1, tau_2). Measurable from
   cortisol and HPA axis response data.

2. CIRCADIAN: Jet lag recovery time T_recovery satisfies
   T_recovery ~ V(perturbation) / (c * (1 - e^{-tau}))
   where V is the phase displacement. Testable against
   existing chronobiology data.

3. HOMEOPATHIC: The dose-response curve of a homeopathic
   remedy administered at intervals Delta_t satisfies the
   stochastic Lyapunov condition with tau computable from
   the allostatic network parameters. Predictions differ
   from standard pharmacological models at sigma ~ tau.
