## Key design decisions

1. **33 is a conjecture everywhere** — stated honestly in Book III (Ch VIII),
   correctly positioned in Book II (g6_scale_invariance.tex), never as theorem.

2. **No cross-volume dependency** — each book introduces its own vocabulary.
   A reader of Book III never needs to open Book I.

3. **Cambridge pitch** — Book III is the submission document. It reads like
   a Cambridge University Press popular science title: Russell-Whitehead
   gravitas, generous definitions, multidisciplinary without condescension.

4. **AXLE is the invitation** — every book ends with an invitation to
   the AXLE repository as the living computational continuation.

5. **G6 LLC as imprint** — all three volumes published under G6 LLC.
   Book III needs a new ISBN (different title from current 979-8-9954416-0-1).
